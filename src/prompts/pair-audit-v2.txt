{"verdict":"pass"|"revise","findings":[{"kind":"reversal"|"omission"|"addition"|"unclear"|"brief"|"editorial","artifact":"detailed"|"brief"|"pair","text":"...","anchor":"D-P001","slot":""}],"readability":[{"observation_id":"R-001","assessment":"acceptable"|"unreadable"|"uncertain","explanation":"..."}]}

Give exactly one readability assessment for every supplied observation id, in
the same order, and invent none. A deterministic observation is a request for
judgment, not an automatic style failure. acceptable means the passage remains
coherent and first-pass readable despite the measurement. unreadable means its
paragraph or sentence structure obscures topic, attribution, sequence,
comparison, conditions, or qualifications. uncertain is blocking until repair
or a fresh review resolves it. Any unreadable or uncertain assessment requires
a corresponding editorial finding anchored to the implicated segment and a
revise verdict.

For ordinary findings, use only controller-issued segment ids and insertion
slots. Report at most eight, most serious first. reversal covers changed hedge,
attribution, uncertainty, causality, chronology, direction, or condition.
omission covers material claims, mechanisms, quantities, conditions,
limitations, disagreements, qualifications, or endings absent from Detailed.
addition covers unsupported content or relations. unclear covers lost meaning,
compression, fragmentation, unmarked topic or actor changes, multiple unrelated
explanatory jobs, overloaded syntax, repetition, or discontinuity. brief covers
contradiction with Detailed or loss of a governing qualification. Missing
headings alone are not defects.

The Brief is a global selection and may omit secondary material. Do not report
an omission from Brief merely because a Detailed unit was not selected.

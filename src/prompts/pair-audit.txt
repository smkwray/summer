Compare the two readings below against the source and report their defects.
{SCOPE}
Return one JSON object and nothing else:

{"verdict": "pass" | "revise", "findings": [{"kind": "...", "artifact": "...", "text": "...", "anchor": "", "slot": ""}]}

Each finding names one specific, fixable defect. kind is reversal, omission,
addition, unclear, or brief. artifact is detailed, brief, or pair. text is
one sentence. Report at most eight, most serious first. If nothing material
is wrong, return "pass" with an empty list; a clean verdict is a real answer.

Use only the controller-issued segment ids and insertion slots listed below.
anchor is the paragraph or heading to change, or "". slot is one of those
slots (after:D-P004, before:B-P001, start) when the defect is an omission

SEGMENTS:

{SEGMENTS}

REVERSAL. A claim the source hedges, qualifies, attributes to some people, or
marks as uncertain, disputed, assumed, correlational, conditional, or
contested, which a reading states plainly; or a settled source claim that a
reading makes sound doubtful. This is the most serious defect.

OMISSION. Something in the source with no counterpart in the Detailed
reading whose loss changes the central argument or conclusion, a causal or
procedural mechanism, a quantity needed to understand scale or comparison, a
condition, limitation, disagreement, or qualification, or the reader's grasp
of what happened and why it matters; an ending the reading never reaches
counts. The loss of an example, a repetition, supporting detail, or an aside
that changes none of these is not an omission: the reading is meant to be
much shorter than the source.

ADDITION. Anything the source does not support: outside background,
evaluation, an implication the source does not draw, a figure attached to
the wrong subject, a changed unit, range, direction, chronology, or
attribution, or a causal or consensus link between facts the source only
places side by side.

UNCLEAR. A passage so compressed or fragmentary that a reader could not
recover its claim without the source. Report this only when meaning is lost,
not when the style is merely plain.

BRIEF. The Brief reading contradicting the Detailed one, asserting something
the Detailed one does not, or carrying a claim over without the qualification
the source attaches to it. The Brief is a selection and is meant to leave
things out; do not report material missing from it.

Do not report length, word choice, or a missing heading, and do not ask for
more as such: say what is missing.
